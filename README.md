# Kinetic Rotating Sculpture

A LEGO Technic kinetic sculpture with a custom gearbox that produces counter-rotating motion from a single motor. The blade designs are inspired by the work of kinetic artist David C. Roy.

[YouTube Demo](https://youtube.com/shorts/mMwfQebC_T4)

## Gearbox Design

The mechanical core is a custom gearbox built from standard LEGO Technic parts. It takes input from a single motor and splits the drive into two counter-rotating outputs. The constraint — achieving smooth, synchronized counter-rotation without a custom drivetrain — makes it a useful study in gearbox design and gear ratio selection.

The sculpture works as a STEM activity for exploring mechanical engineering principles hands-on.

## Blade Designs

Two blade designs are included, both drawn from David C. Roy's kinetic work. The files are provided as `.svg` and `.eps` and can be opened in Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, or most vector editing software.
